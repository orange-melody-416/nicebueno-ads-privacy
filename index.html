Privacy Policy

Nicebueno Ads Analytics Tool
Last Updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nicebueno Ads Analytics Tool (the "Application") accesses, uses, retains, and protects information obtained through Amazon Advertising APIs and related Amazon authorization services.

1. Purpose and Scope

The Application is an internal business tool used solely with our own Amazon seller and advertising accounts. It supports internal Amazon Advertising data analysis, account management, technical testing, and debugging. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide services to third-party Amazon sellers or advertisers.

2. Information We Access

Subject to the permissions granted by our authorized account users and the APIs available to the Application, we may access:

  • Advertising account and profile identifiers, campaigns, ad groups, keywords, and search terms.
  • Advertising performance metrics, including impressions, clicks, spend, attributed sales, and attributed conversions.
  • Campaign settings, budgets, bids, and advertising reports.
  • API credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, and limited technical diagnostic information necessary to authorize API requests and troubleshoot the Application.

The Application is not designed to collect Amazon customer names, customer contact details, payment card information, or other customer-level personal information.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only for our internal business operations involving our own accounts, including performance analysis, campaign monitoring, keyword and search term analysis, bid optimization, budget management, reporting, and technical testing and debugging. Authorization information is used to establish and maintain permitted API access.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or disclose Amazon advertising data to third parties. Advertising data is accessible only to authorized personnel for the internal purposes described in this policy. It is not published on this website or in the public source-code repository.

5. Credentials and Security

API credentials, access tokens, and refresh tokens are treated as confidential. They must not be included in public repositories, public webpages, shared reports, or publicly accessible logs. Access to advertising data and authorization information is restricted to authorized personnel who need it to operate the Application.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure, including access controls and secure transmission when communicating with Amazon APIs. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain advertising data and technical diagnostic information only for as long as needed for the internal purposes described above, subject to applicable Amazon requirements and legal obligations. Data that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymized. Authorization credentials and tokens are removed when no longer required, including when the relevant integration is discontinued. Any retained backups are subject to the same access restrictions and are removed through the backup retention process.

Authorized account users may revoke access through the applicable Amazon account authorization controls and may contact us to request deletion of data held by the Application. Revoking access stops future authorized retrieval but does not itself delete previously retained information.

7. Third-Party Services

The Application interacts with Amazon Advertising APIs and related Amazon authorization services. Those services are governed by Amazon's own terms and privacy notices.

This public privacy notice is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard website request information, such as visitors' IP addresses and browser information, under its own privacy statement. The public page contains no Amazon advertising data, API credentials, access tokens, or refresh tokens. We do not add advertising trackers, analytics scripts, or cookies to this page.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the Application or its data practices. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
